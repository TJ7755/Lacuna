# Recover Question Set Concepts

Keep a full export of the affected device and obtain an intact full backup from
before the damaging update or another paired device. Update both devices first.
Use Settings’ **Combine two devices** with the intact file, or sync with the intact
device. Check the recovered course and export a fresh full backup afterwards.

If recovery fails, preserve both exports and look for an older intact backup. Do
not delete Question Sets or edit deletion receipts to force sync through.
