# Product principles

- Prioritise useful practice towards the learner’s assessments.
- Let learners keep moving through useful study modes towards predicted exam-day readiness across
  all their assessment material, including earlier unstudied material when joining part-way.
  Due dates guide practice rather than define its scope; preserve FSRS-based memory evidence and
  spacing, and distinguish a pause in useful practice from reaching the readiness target.
- Keep study available without an account or network.
- Support keyboard and touch access.
- Present predictions as estimates, never guarantees of examination marks.
