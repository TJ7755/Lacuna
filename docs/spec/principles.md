# Product principles

- Scheduling towards an assessment and reporting predicted readiness must use the same objective.
  Session completion is a separate measure of work done.
- Cards and Questions share knowledge identities, not learning evidence. One must not silently
  change the other's scheduling or recorded answers.
- Study remains local and usable without an account or network. Optional sync and AI have explicit
  transport and authority boundaries; selected AI content may leave the device.
- Touch and keyboard workflows are equally supported. Motion confirms state changes without
  delaying input, concealing controls or replacing accessible feedback.
- Predictions are model estimates, not guarantees of recall, mastery or examination marks.
