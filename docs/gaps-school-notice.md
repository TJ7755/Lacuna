# School trial notice preparation

Before involving pupils, agree a data notice with the school covering consent,
identifiable study records, hosting metadata, retention, withdrawal, teacher-held
copies and recovery responsibilities. Verify every service claim against the
reviewed deployed source before distributing the notice. Do not treat this
repository as the school’s approved privacy notice.

Use the [trial operations procedure](gaps-trial-operations.md) and rehearse with
synthetic records. Ask school IT about profile clearing and keep recovery material
outside disposable profiles. Never publish pupil records or credentials in issues.
