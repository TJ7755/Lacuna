## Lacuna 0.2.15 Beta

- A redesigned workspace across Today, courses, lessons, Cards, Search, Progress and Settings, with clearer actions, consistent light and dark themes, and bundled fonts for offline use.
- Study opens from a compact session plan. Cards fit the available window, long answers scroll within the card, images arrive at the right size, and touch gestures and grading transitions respect reduced motion.
- Faster repeat entry for Cards, Questions, sequences and diagram regions, with clearer field errors, keyboard shortcuts and focus handling. Question Set attempts keep answers, marking and reflection together.
- Search shows matching excerpts and live counts, keeps filters and your place when returning from a result, and brings keyboard-selected results into view.
- Course-update reviews explain Current and Incoming content and the effect of each choice. Pairing and recovery put the next action first, with technical details available when needed.
- Improved narrow-window layouts, larger-text support, touch targets and keyboard navigation throughout the redesigned screens.
- Study starts with fewer repeated reads, Quick search loads on demand, and unchanged device-sync snapshots avoid unnecessary database replacement while retaining safety snapshots for changes.

Export a full backup before updating. This release retains the existing database and backup formats used by 0.2.14; existing desktop profiles and study records are retained on upgrade. Keep devices that sync on the same current version.
