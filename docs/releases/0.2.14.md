## Lacuna 0.2.14 Beta

- Question Sets: write exam-style papers with nested parts, mark schemes, diagrams and links to concepts and cards, and add them to a course as Practice Qs after a lesson. Attempts track coverage and marks separately from card scheduling.
- Simple Learn teaches poems, speeches and scripts by cumulative recitation, typed or aloud, and leaving a session keeps your place.
- Mermaid diagrams render in notes and cards, `[[Note name]]` links show as pills, and wide tables scroll.
- Large libraries sync again: sync snapshots are compressed before encryption. Syncing no longer pulls review dates forward, and manual reschedules survive it.
- Due counts agree everywhere, a multi-lesson session respects the course's daily new-card limit once, and revision plans keep finished days closed when an exam changes.
- Typed answers: 5 no longer matches -5, accents compare correctly, and confirming an input-method candidate no longer reveals the card.
- Anki imports no longer push review cards hundreds of days into the future, and many text, CSV, TSV and Markdown import edge cases are fixed. Exported spreadsheets can no longer run formulas.
- Audio cards play again, maths renders with proper fraction lines, and the Pomodoro timer keeps accurate time in the background.
- Security: the desktop app sets Electron fuses and a tighter content security policy, the AI companion socket moved to your private profile folder on macOS and Linux, and the relay only answers the Lacuna apps.

Update every device you sync: earlier versions cannot read the new compressed sync snapshots. Backups made by this version do not open in 0.2.13. Existing desktop profiles and study records are retained on upgrade.
