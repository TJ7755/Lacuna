Arc 6 — occlusion, chosen design

The label-box model. One annotated image produces two kinds of card, because a rectangle carries a role: a label box sits over text already printed on the diagram, and a feature region points at a thing in the drawing. Nothing here asks the author to retype a label that is already in the picture. Click a ringed region to reveal.

Study

Both card kinds mask every label box on the front, so the diagram can never answer itself by elimination. What differs is what the front asks and what the back uncovers.

Typed mode needs an authored label, so it is offered only on regions that have one.
One card per feature region. Front rings a part of the drawing with all labels masked; the back uncovers the paired label box. Pairing is what keeps this typing-free too. Where a region has no pair (an unlabelled diagram), the author supplies the text and the back shows that instead.
