on 27 September 2026, at a 1440 × 960 CSS-pixel viewport. The browser capture service
exports 1280 × 853 PNGs. Images are shown at their original aspect ratio and can be
expanded; no interface elements, review outcomes or progress figures were composited.

The separate `127.0.0.1:5174` origin contains the fresh built-in Welcome to Lacuna
example course. The announcement was dismissed using its normal control.

- `course.png`: course path and selected lesson.
- `lesson.png`: first lesson and its notes.
- `settings.png`: course settings and study controls.
- `recall.png`: first Simple Learn prompt after continuing past the introductory note.

No cards were graded. These are real application views of example material, not
screenshots of a learner's private course. The interactive page illustrations are
separate from these captures and are labelled as examples.
