2026, at a 1440 × 960 CSS-pixel viewport and device scale factor 2. The lossless
PNGs are 2880 × 1920 pixels, rendered at device scale after fonts loaded, with
reduced motion. They replace the downsampled 1280 × 853 browser-preview exports.

review outcomes or progress figures were composited or upscaled.

control. No existing browser profile or learner data was used.

screenshots of a learner's private course. The interactive page illustrations are
